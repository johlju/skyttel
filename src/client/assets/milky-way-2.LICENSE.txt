Så används de fyra källorna i bilden

- COBE/DIRBE mäter himlens infraröda ljus. Mätningarna ger stjärnbandet
  dess ljusfördelning, från den ljusa galaxkärnan till de svagare delarna.
- Edenhofers tredimensionella stoftkarta visar hur långt bort närliggande
  stoftmoln finns. Det gör att vi kan dämpa ljuset från stjärnorna bakom dem.
- SFD98 anger den sammanlagda mängden stoft i varje riktning. Kartan ger
  de mörka stråken i Vintergatan finare detaljer.
- Stjärnkatalogen HYG ger de ljusstarkaste stjärnorna deras positioner,
  ljusstyrkor och färger.

COBE/DIRBE near-infrared sky brightness

Credit: NASA Goddard Space Flight Center and the COBE Science Working Group.
The 2.2-micron Zodi-Subtracted Mission Average data provide the large-scale
stellar light distribution. These US government data are publicly available.
https://lambda.gsfc.nasa.gov/product/cobe/dirbe_products.html
https://lambda.gsfc.nasa.gov/product/cobe/dirbe_zsma_data_get.html
Intensity and coordinate files: DIRBE_BAND2A_ZSMA.FITS and DIRBE_SKYMAP_INFO.FITS.
https://lambda.gsfc.nasa.gov/product/cobe/dirbe_ancil_qs_get.html

Local three-dimensional interstellar dust

Credit: Gordian Edenhofer, Catherine Zucker, Philipp Frank, Andrew K.
Saydjari, Joshua S. Speagle, Douglas Finkbeiner and Torsten Ensslin.
Edenhofer et al. (2023), A Parsec-Scale Galactic 3D Dust Map out to
1.25 kpc from the Sun. Numerical dataset, doi:10.5281/zenodo.8212070.
The local dust density is integrated along sightlines to model foreground
attenuation. Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.
https://zenodo.org/records/8212070
Input file: mean_and_std_healpix.fits.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Galactic reddening

Credit: David J. Schlegel, Douglas P. Finkbeiner and Marc Davis.
Schlegel, Finkbeiner & Davis (1998), Maps of Dust Infrared Emission for
Use in Estimation of Reddening and Cosmic Microwave Background Radiation
Foregrounds, ApJ 500, 525. Numerical dataset, doi:10.7910/DVN/EWCNL5.
The SFD98 map supplies the full dust column and finer angular structure.
The Harvard Dataverse distribution is dedicated under CC0 1.0.
https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/EWCNL5
Input files: SFD_dust_4096_ngp.fits and SFD_dust_4096_sgp.fits.
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Bright-star catalogue

Credit: David Nash / Astronexus, HYG v4.1.
The rendering uses measured positions, visual magnitudes and colour indices
from a subset of this catalogue. Coordinates are converted to a Galactic
frame and the stars are rendered into the panorama.
Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International.
https://github.com/astronexus/HYG-Database/blob/main/hyg/README.md
Input file: hyg_v41.csv, in the catalogue's hyg/CURRENT directory.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

The rendering converts these measurements into a visible-light-looking
illustration through exposure, colour, dust-transmission and fine stellar
detail choices. Infrared observations are interpreted for visual appearance.
The resulting colours and brightness are an artistic representation.
